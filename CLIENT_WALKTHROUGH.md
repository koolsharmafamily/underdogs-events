# Underdogs — Client Walkthrough & Experience Guide

Welcome to the newly refined **Underdogs** digital experience (Desktop Website & Mobile Web App). 

Built to celebrate **Two Worlds, Two Celebrations, One Unforgettable Season**, the experience has been elevated with world-class art direction, bespoke typography, continuous 3D badge choreography, zero-latency physical interactions, and cinematic lighting.

## 🌟 What's New Across the Experience

### 1. Home · "Gate to Rage"
- **3D Enamel Badge Hero**: Centered with chrome edge highlights, real-time lighting response, winks, tongue wags, and interactive drag-to-spin physics.
- **Statement Turns (H2 · Who We Are)**: As you scroll through the three core statements, the badge dynamically pivots:
  - *Statement 1*: Turns edge-on (72°) showcasing high-relief enamel depth.
  - *Statement 2*: Turns 180° revealing the engraved reverse plate.
  - *Statement 3*: Snaps home face-front (360°).
- **"This Season" Diptych (H4)**: Newly reinstated desktop feature showcasing the official posters for *The Red Room* and *DHAMAAKA* in tailored metallic frames with direct portal transitions.
- **Atmospheric Shafts & Glossy Floor**: Signature magenta beams in volumetric fog with a high-definition glossy floor reflection.
- **Grounded Portals (H5)**: Two physical 3D doors standing on the floor threshold with authentic materials and illuminated light leaks under the sills.

## 🗺️ How to Navigate & Review

1. **Gate to Rage (Home)**:
   - Visit `/`
   - Scroll through the hero to witness the badge statement turns.
   - Preview the "This season" diptych.
   - Hover the doors at the bottom to watch the badge tilt and look toward the doorway.
2. **The Red Room**:
   - Click "Enter World One" or choose *The Red Room* in the navigation bar.
   - Observe the smooth curtain entrance, the smoke dragon, and the mirror condensation.
3. **DHAMAAKA**:
   - Navigate to `/diwali` from the nav or the golden arch door.
   - Experience the clapperboard snap, the jewelled title sheen, and the marquee lightbox.
4. **Mobile Web App**:
   - Open on your mobile phone or toggle device simulation in DevTools (390×844).
   - Test tab switching between **Home** and **Events**, card interactions, and top bar back navigation.
